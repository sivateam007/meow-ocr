About Meow OCR

A simple, free, privacy-first OCR tool built with 🐱 and a lot of coffee.

Who's Behind It

Meow OCR was created and developed by Siva, an independent developer who wanted a genuinely free, no-strings-attached way to extract text from documents. No signup walls. No credit card. No watermark. Just upload, convert, and go.

Why It Exists

Most OCR tools hide their best features behind paid plans or force you to create accounts before you've done anything. Meow OCR flips that — it's completely free to use, supports 19+ languages (including Tamil, Hindi, and other Indian languages), and works right in your browser. Whether it's a Tamil textbook page, a Hindi form, or an English invoice, Meow OCR turns it into editable text in seconds.

Your Privacy Matters

Your documents are processed using the free Tesseract OCR engine and stored temporarily in secure cloud storage. By default, files are automatically deleted after 2 days for your privacy. No account is required to use the core tool.

The Free Promise

  • Free forever — no hidden costs
  • No signup required for the core tool
  • No card — ever
  • No watermark on your extracted text
  • 19+ languages supported
  • Results auto-delete after 2 days by default
