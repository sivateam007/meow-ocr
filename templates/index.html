Tips for best results

  • Handwritten notes may cause errors. If your file was OCR'd from handwriting, the extracted text can be uncertain — mistranslations are expected.
  • For the most accurate translation, use clear, typed, clean text with correct spelling.
  • Short, simple sentences translate more reliably than long, complex ones.
  • Check the source language is selected correctly — a wrong choice can garble the result.
  • Special characters, emojis, or unusual formatting may not translate well.
  • Always proofread the translated output before using it for important work.

Frequently Asked Questions 🐾

Is Meow OCR really free?

Yes. Converting files to text is completely free with no signup for your first scan, and unlimited after a free Google sign-in.

Which languages are supported?

Tamil, English, Hindi, Telugu, Bengali, Kannada, Malayalam, Gujarati, Punjabi, Marathi, Arabic, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Russian, Chinese, Japanese and Korean — with auto-detection.

Can I upload large files?

Yes. Large multi-page PDFs are processed in batches to stay fast, so even big documents convert smoothly.

Are my files kept private?

Files are processed securely on the server and automatically deleted from cloud storage after 2 days (privacy auto-delete). No account or personal details are needed for basic use.

Can Meow OCR read my handwritten notes?

Yes. The Handwritten Notes scanner uses a dedicated AI vision model to read photos of handwriting — Tamil, Hindi, English and more — and gives you clean editable text instantly. You'll find it right below the upload section on the homepage.

Can I use Meow OCR on mobile?

Absolutely. The whole tool is mobile-responsive and works great on phones and tablets.
