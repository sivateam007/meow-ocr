Link to Us & Share Meow OCR

A backlink is simply another website linking to ours - it helps people find Meow OCR and boosts our search ranking. Here is everything you can copy, paste or share in under a minute.

1. Add the badge to your website

Paste this snippet anywhere on your site (blog sidebar, tools page, footer). The badge links back to Meow OCR.

2. Share on social media

One click opens a pre-filled post. Every share is a link back to the tool.

Post on X Share on Facebook Share on LinkedIn WhatsApp

3. Recommended anchor text

Use descriptive anchor text when you link - it helps search engines understand the page. Copy one of these examples:

  • Free OCR - extract text from PDF and images online
  • Tamil OCR without signup, in seconds
  • A free tool to convert PDF to editable text
  • Meow OCR - simple, private, browser-based OCR

4. Free directories & places to submit

Listing in a directory gives you a real backlink. Stick to legitimate, free list-based sites (search "[name] submit tool" and use the official form):

  • Tool / app listing sites such as Product Hunt (launch post), AlternativeTo, and SaaSHub
  • Free web directory categories for "Productivity Tools" or "Text & OCR"
  • Startup & maker hubs like Indie Hackers and Hacker News "Show HN"
  • Quora/Reddit/forums - answer "how do I extract text from a scanned PDF?" and link naturally (no spam)
  • Your own profiles: GitHub README, Twitter/X bio, LinkedIn, or a WordPress.com blog

5. Quick rules for safe backlinks

  • Only submit to sites you can post on manually - relevance matters more than volume.
  • Never buy links or use spam networks; Google can penalise that.
  • Add rel="noopener" and (for paid links only) rel="nofollow".
  • Slow, steady growth beats a sudden spike. A few quality links a month is ideal.
