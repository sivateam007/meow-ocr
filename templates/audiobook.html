Audiobook Maker — FAQs

Is the Meow OCR audiobook maker really free?
Yes. Converting documents, images or pasted text into narrated MP3 audiobooks is completely free — no signup, no watermark, and no hidden limits on your files or voices.
How do I convert a PDF into an audiobook?
In the PDF & Documents → Audiobook MP3 section above, upload your file, pick the document language and narrator voice, then press Convert to Audiobook MP3. Meow OCR reads the text out of your file (scanned pages are handled with built-in OCR) and narrates it in the background. When it's done, the MP3 appears in My Downloads.
Which languages and voices are supported?
19+ languages for reading documents and 45+ natural neural voices — English, Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, Bengali, Kannada, Malayalam, Gujarati, Marathi, Arabic, Spanish, French, German and many more. Use Auto-pick and Meow OCR chooses the best voice for the detected language automatically.
Can the narrator sound like me?
Yes. Upload a short voice clip in the My Voice section below. Meow OCR detects your pitch and speaking speed, then matches the closest built-in voice and tunes its speed and pitch to you. Apply it to the Text to Speech tool and save it onto the PDF tool with one click.
