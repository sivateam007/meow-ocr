How to Use Meow OCR

Paw-fect guide by our senior cat engineer 🐱

Convert any PDF, image or document to editable text in under a minute. Follow these 3 simple steps.

Step 1 — Upload Your File

Click inside the upload box (or drag & drop) to choose your file. Meow OCR supports PDFs, scanned images (JPG, PNG, BMP, TIFF), documents (DOCX, PPTX, RTF, ODT) and data files (XLSX, CSV, XML, JSON). Large files are processed in batches, so even big documents convert without crashing.

Tip: Large PDFs are processed in batches, so even 100-page books convert without crashing.

Step 2 — Choose Options

Pick the document's language (or leave it on Auto-Detect for 19+ languages). Optionally select a page range if you only need part of a PDF, and set your preferred auto-delete time for total privacy.

Tip: Keep Auto-Delete on 2 Days — your cloud files vanish automatically for privacy.

Step 3 — Download Your Text

Hit Extract Text and you'll be taken to a live progress page. When done, grab your editable .txt file from My Downloads. You can also share a secure link.

Tip: Sign in with Google for unlimited conversions and the ability to recall past results.

What Formats Does Meow OCR Support?

PDF JPG / JPEG PNG BMP TIFF GIF DOCX / DOC PPTX / PPT XLSX / XLS / CSV RTF ODT / ODS / ODP HTML / XML / JSON

Which Languages Are Supported?

Meow OCR auto-detects and extracts text in 19+ languages including Tamil, English, Hindi, Telugu, Bengali, Kannada, Malayalam, Gujarati, Punjabi, Marathi, Arabic, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Russian, Chinese, Japanese and Korean.

Privacy Auto-Delete

Your uploaded files and results are stored securely in the cloud. By default they are automatically deleted after 2 days. You can choose 1, 7 or 30 days, or keep them forever. This keeps your private documents safe.
